# NuraCare 2.0 — Global Wellness Intelligence Platform

## Project Overview

Redesign and upgrade NuraCare from a reactive symptom-checking platform into a proactive, AI-powered Wellness Intelligence Platform.

## Old Reactive Model

```text
Symptom Onset
      ↓
Manual Input
      ↓
Basic Urgency Assessment
      ↓
Natural Remedy Suggestion
```

## New Proactive Model

```text
Wearables + Lifestyle Habits
            ↓
AI Wellness Intelligence Engine
            ↓
Predictive Health Scores
            ↓
Preventive Recommendations
            ↓
Behavior Change
            ↓
Improved Wellness Outcomes
```
